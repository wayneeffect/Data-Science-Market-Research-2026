**Tips for choosing**:
- For **high-volume/contract/staff augmentation** — Insight Global, TEKsystems, or Robert Half scale well.
- For **specialized/senior data science or ML roles** — Boutiques like Redfish, Harnham, Nexus IT Group, Burtch Works, or Averity provide deeper technical vetting and better matches.
- Data science hiring prioritizes skills in Python/R, SQL, ML frameworks, cloud platforms (AWS/Azure/GCP), and domain experience. Ask about technical assessments, time-to-fill, retention rates, and replacement guarantees.
- Many agencies offer nearshore options due to US talent shortages. Check reviews on Clutch, Glassdoor, or industry lists.

The data science market evolves rapidly with AI integration. Contact agencies directly via their websites for current capabilities.
