**The global data science market** is most commonly measured through the **Data Science Platform Market** (software/tools/platforms for data science workflows, ML, analytics, etc.). It is a high-growth segment within the broader data analytics and AI ecosystem.

### Current Market Size (as of 2025–2026)
Estimates vary by research firm due to differences in scope (e.g., platforms only vs. services + software), but here are consistent recent figures:

- **Data Science Platform Market**: Approximately **$150–$175 billion in 2025**, rising to roughly **$200–$220 billion in 2026**.
  - Examples: One report cites $153.71 billion in 2025 → $204.05 billion in 2026.
  - Another: ~$171–$175 billion in 2025.

- **Broader Data Analytics Market** (which includes data science activities): Around **$65–$85 billion in 2025**, with 2026 projections in the **$100–$110 billion** range.

These figures reflect spending on platforms, tools, services, cloud resources, and related technologies driving data science work.

### Projections and Growth
The market is experiencing explosive growth, fueled by AI adoption, big data volumes, cloud computing, and demand for real-time analytics.

- **Data Science Platform Market**:
  - Expected to reach **$470–$700+ billion by 2030**, with some forecasts up to **$600–$975 billion by 2034**.
  - **CAGR**: Typically **20–33%** annually through the early 2030s (e.g., 32.6% in one projection to 2030, 20.4% in another to 2034).

- **Data Analytics Market** (broader):
  - Projected to grow to **$300–$500+ billion by 2030–2034**, with longer-term estimates exceeding **$700–$1,000 billion by 2035** in optimistic scenarios.
  - CAGR: Often **21–28%+**.

**Key drivers** include generative AI integration, MLOps adoption, enterprise digital transformation, and demand in industries like tech, finance, healthcare, retail, and manufacturing. North America (especially the US) dominates, followed by strong growth in Asia-Pacific.

**Note on scope**: These are platform/software market sizes. The **overall economic impact** of data science (including jobs, efficiency gains, and AI value creation) is much larger—often estimated in trillions when factoring in downstream business value. Related big data analytics markets are also in the hundreds of billions and growing rapidly.

**Bottom line**: The data science platform market is currently worth **around $150–200 billion** (2025–2026) and is on track for massive expansion to **hundreds of billions** by 2030, with sustained double-digit to low-30% CAGRs. It remains one of the fastest-growing technology sectors. For the most precise numbers, refer to reports from firms like Fortune Business Insights, Grand View Research, or Precedence Research, as definitions and estimates evolve.
